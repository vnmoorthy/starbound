Open the public URL and the PowerPoint. For live inference, start the local app and `npm run lab`, open its temporary URL, and verify Codex login. ## Suggested narration

**00:00 — The question.** “Could an AI propose a star-scale engineering plan and survive an independent reality check? This is StarBound. It follows Mercury materials into a solar swarm and calculates how much electricity reaches Earth.”

**00:30 — Thermal failure.** Move the collectors to 0.3 AU with radiator ratio 0.5. “Closer means more sunlight, but this design exceeds its temperature limit. The engine stops counting its electrical output.” Restore radiator ratio 2.

**01:00 — Transmission.** Reset and compare direct microwave with optical relay. “Harvesting is not delivery. This link budget includes diffraction, conversion and receiver limits. These specific apertures give dramatically different results.”

**01:35 — Astra's decision.** Open the labeled recorded experiment or the completed live run. Apply its low-mining policy. “Astra saw the receiver saturating. It reduced factory expansion. The engine—not the model—computed the power and the saved material.”

**02:20 — Evidence and ambition.** Show the comparison. “Sol and ordinary search reached the same mining score. This first task does not prove an Astra-only capability. What we've built is an open testbench: every future claim can be challenged against the same physics and unseen failures.”

**02:45 — The useful next step.** “The practical output today is a laboratory for engineering reasoning. The next release adds real orbital geometry, lead times, receiver networks and uncertainty. You can reproduce every result from this repository.”

## Likely questions

**Can we actually build this?** Not from this model alone. It explicitly assumes technologies and starting infrastructure that require substantial research. The roadmap names each gate.

**Why Mercury?** The concept explores accessible surface feedstock, intense sunlight and its escape-energy scale. It does not claim the core is readily mined or transport from Earth is cheap.

**What does Astra do?** Propose, interpret numerical feedback and revise a bounded policy. The host engine calculates and grades. Real model outputs and all three rounds are available.

**Why not Sol?** Sol can do this task and tied on the declared mining score. We do not make an unsupported exclusion claim.

**Does this solve climate change?** No. The power-use examples are conditional energy equivalents. Economics, lifecycle emissions, infrastructure and alternative technologies must be evaluated.

**Is the swarm collision-safe?** That is not established. The display samples circular orbits and the numerical model is aggregate. Collision-free trajectories are follow-on work.

## What would strengthen the submission further

Observe an independent viewer using the app without coaching. Add a held-out, predeclared fault scenario and repeat model trials with robust thermal margins. Obtain a domain expert's review of the boundaries. Record a concise demo video if the actual submission form requires one. Do not fabricate demand, results, endorsements, views or stars.
