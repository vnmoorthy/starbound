# Third-party software and generated assets

StarBound is authored by `vnmoorthy` with AI assistance. Its initial Git history contains no imported third-party commit history. Dependencies and scaffold-derived components retain their own licenses; author attribution does not transfer ownership of dependencies.

The project uses the official Sites/Vinext scaffold, React, Three.js, shadcn/Base UI primitives, Lucide icons, Tailwind CSS, Vite and Cloudflare tooling. Exact versions and dependency provenance are in `package-lock.json`; license texts are included in the installed packages. The project-local Codex CLI is distributed by OpenAI under its own terms and license.

The two images under `public/assets/` are original AI-generated engineering concepts made with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. Their complete prompts are in `docs/IMAGE-PROMPTS.txt`. They are not spacecraft/factory photographs or validated hardware designs. No SpaceX logo or proprietary SpaceX imagery is used.

Scientific sources are linked in `docs/RESEARCH.md` and `docs/PHYSICS.md`. Their authors do not endorse this project. No papers or video transcripts are redistributed.

The presentation uses Helvetica Neue where available. Font files are not included or redistributed.
